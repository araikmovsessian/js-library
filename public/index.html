    The route

    How a journey comes together

    1. A conversation

      An hour by phone or in person. We ask how you like to travel, what you have loved before and what you would rather avoid.

    2. A journey, drawn up

      Within a week you receive a day-by-day plan with rooms, guides and prices. We revise it until it is right.

    3. Travel, looked after

      Your planner stays on call from departure to return, and quietly fixes whatever needs fixing.
